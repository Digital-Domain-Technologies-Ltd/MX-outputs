MX Content

COG content delivery for the Machine Experience ecosystem

This endpoint serves COG content files published by registered MX publishers. Content is resolved through REGINALD, the MX COG registry.

Content Paths

  • GET /cogs/{namespace}/{name}/ — Published COG content

How It Works

  1. Query REGINALD for a COG pointer record
  2. The pointer's canonical_url points to content on this domain
  3. Fetch the content directly from the canonical URL
