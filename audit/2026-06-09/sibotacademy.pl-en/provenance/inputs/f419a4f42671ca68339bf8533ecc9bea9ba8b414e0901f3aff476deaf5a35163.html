What age is Sibot Academy suitable for?

Sibot Academy is designed for children aged 7–14.

No. Sibot Academy has no open chat. All activities are controlled and safe — children cannot freely write to AI.

Yes. Parents have a simple dashboard showing completed lessons and their child's progress.

One lesson takes approximately 10–15 minutes.
