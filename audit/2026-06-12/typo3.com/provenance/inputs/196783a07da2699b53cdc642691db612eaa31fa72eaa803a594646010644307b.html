Features

Seamless Connectivity. Unmatched Scalability.

Multisite and Multilingual

TYPO3 simplifies multilingual site management with translation workflows and integration. It supports unlimited websites and languages for global businesses.

Security & Predictable Releases

TYPO3 is built with security as a core principle, ensuring robust protection for your data through dedicated security teams and strict access controls. With predictable release cycles, you can rely on regular updates and long-term support, keeping your system secure and up to date without unexpected disruptions.

Learn More About Security

Enterprise-Grade Digital Solutions

TYPO3 is built for enterprise-scale: managing tens or hundreds of sites, high performance under load, seamless integration with ERP/CRM systems, and dedicated vendor support. Organisations with global reach and mission-critical demands trust TYPO3 for its infrastructure and ecosystem.

Discover Enterprise Capabilities

Accessible by Design — Inclusive for Everyone

TYPO3 is designed with accessibility in mind: the backend supports keyboard navigation and screen reader use, while frontend projects can meet WCAG 2.1 and national standards to serve users of all abilities.

See Accessibility Standards
