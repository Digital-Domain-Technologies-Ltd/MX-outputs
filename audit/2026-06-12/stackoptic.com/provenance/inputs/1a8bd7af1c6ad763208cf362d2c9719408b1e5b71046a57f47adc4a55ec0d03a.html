Common questions

About StackOptic

What does StackOptic analyze?
StackOptic runs 18 specialized modules on any publicly accessible website from a single URL: technology stack (7,000+ signatures), Core Web Vitals performance, SSL & HTTP security, SEO meta & structured data, email authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), AI & LLM readiness (llms.txt, bot access, GEO score), accessibility, privacy compliance, carbon footprint, and more.
How is StackOptic different from Wappalyzer or BuiltWith?
Wappalyzer and BuiltWith focus on technology detection. StackOptic adds Lighthouse-calibrated performance scoring, security grading, SEO auditing, AI readiness analysis for LLM search engines, and an AI-written technical deep-dive — all in one report, powered by a real browser that bypasses bot protection.

Is there a free plan?
Yes. The Free plan includes 5 analyses per day, all 18 modules, 7,000+ technology detection, security grades, and permanent shareable reports — forever, no credit card required.
Does StackOptic work on sites behind Cloudflare?
Yes. StackOptic uses a real browser engine (Patchright / Camoufox) with a residential proxy pool that successfully analyzes sites protected by Cloudflare, Sucuri, LiteSpeed Cache, and similar systems.
What is AI & LLM readiness?
A 100-point score across 4 categories (Bot Access, Machine Readability, Structured Data, Citation Readiness) that measures how visible and citable your site is to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and other AI search engines. It audits 8 major AI bots, validates llms.txt, and scores JSON-LD quality.
Do I own my analysis reports?
Yes. Every report is permanently hosted at stackoptic.com/site/[domain] with a unique shareable URL. Paid plans can export PDF and JSON for offline use, white-label, or integration.
