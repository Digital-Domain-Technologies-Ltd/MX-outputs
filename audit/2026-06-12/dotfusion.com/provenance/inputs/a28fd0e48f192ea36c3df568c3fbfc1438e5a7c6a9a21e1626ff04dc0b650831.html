What is headless CMS development?

Headless CMS development separates content management from content presentation, using API-first architecture. This allows content to be distributed across multiple channels (websites, apps, IoT devices) from a single source. It provides greater flexibility, scalability, and enables composable architecture for enterprise digital experiences.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) ensures your content appears in AI-powered search results from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and other AI answer engines. It involves structured data markup (schema), content architecture, and optimization strategies that help AI engines understand, extract, and cite your content as authoritative sources.

What is agentic content operations?

Agentic content operations use AI agents to automate and scale content production workflows. This includes content research, drafting, optimization, metadata generation, and distribution. It allows content teams to multiply their output 5-10x while maintaining quality and brand consistency.
