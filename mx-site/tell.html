What inspect does

Inspect opens the MX Inspector and fetches the PDF into your browser, where it is read locally and never uploaded. It shows the MX metadata embedded in the file, whether the PDF is tagged for assistive technology, the fingerprint of the source it was built from, and the provenance records that travel beside it, which you can follow as an evidence chain.
