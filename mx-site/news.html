Industry News

Recent Developments

Anthropic Launches Fable 5 and Mythos 5, Then Suspends Foreign Access Within 72 Hours

12 June 2026 | Governance

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 on 9 June 2026, its most capable models, available on the Claude API, AWS Bedrock, Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry. Fable 5 includes safety classifiers that can decline requests and return a refusal response rather than an error. Three days later, a US government export control directive required Anthropic to suspend all access by foreign nationals to both models, citing a discovered jailbreak method for cybersecurity tasks. Anthropic complied while publicly disputing the basis, arguing the vulnerability exists across other deployed models. The event is the first documented case of a government directive disabling a globally deployed AI model overnight.

Visa Plugs Its Payment Network Into ChatGPT

10 June 2026 | Commerce

At its Payments Forum in San Francisco, Visa announced it has embedded its payment network inside ChatGPT. Agents can complete purchases at potentially any merchant that accepts the card: OpenAI supplies the agent, Visa the approval and fraud monitoring. The move follows the retirement of OpenAI's opt-in Instant Checkout and inverts the model, reaching merchants who never integrated anything.

Microsoft Launches MAI Models and Frontier Tuning

2 June 2026 | Platforms

Microsoft AI shipped a family of seven in-house MAI models trained from scratch, alongside Frontier Tuning, which adapts a model inside reinforcement-learning environments wired to a company's real workflows. The trace of real work becomes the training asset; a frontier health model co-built with Mayo Clinic runs first inside the hospital's own environment.

Common Crawl Publishes the AI Visibility Audit

1 June 2026 | Ecosystem

The foundation whose archive sits underneath most model training data published a field guide with a blunt central claim: a page can rank in search and still be missing from the models, because reachability by the crawlers that feed training sits upstream of every on-page tactic. The crawl still leans heavily toward English, at roughly 41% of the most recent crawl.

Magnifica Humanitas, the First Papal Encyclical on AI

25 May 2026 | Governance

Pope Leo XIV published the first papal encyclical on artificial intelligence, signed 135 years to the day after Rerum Novarum addressed the rights of workers in the industrial revolution. The choice of date is the argument: this is the industrial revolution again, and the question is who the technology is for and who answers for it.

Google Ships Universal Cart and Takes UCP to Production

19 May 2026 | Commerce

Google moved the Universal Commerce Protocol from pilot to production, shipping Universal Cart with a retail cohort that includes Target, Walmart, and a payments bench spanning Mastercard, Visa, and American Express. AP2 records the structured detail of an agent-mediated purchase; interoperability with the rival ACP remains claimed rather than verified.

EU AI Act Digital Omnibus Defers High-Risk Deadlines

May 2026 | Regulation

The EU's Digital Omnibus deferred the AI Act's high-risk compliance deadlines, easing the near-term timetable without changing the direction: organisations that deploy AI systems will be expected to evidence what those systems did and under which policy. The record-keeping disciplines reward early adoption whatever the final dates.
