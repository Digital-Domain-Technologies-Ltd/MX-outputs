Request an Audit | Ask for a Machine Readability Audit of your site

23 October 2026 · Tom Cranstoun · 2 min read

Give us your web address and a way to reach you. We run the Machine Readability Audit on our own engine, a person reads the report before it goes anywhere, and you hear from us when it is ready.

The audit reads your pages the way a machine does: a search engine, a model crawler, an agent acting for a customer. It measures what each one can read, where it has to guess, and what to change. One run covers machine readability, GEO, SEO and AI tells, because they are measured from the same pages.

How much to audit

What happens next

  1. Your request waits in our queue. Nothing runs until our engine picks it up, so a mistaken address costs nothing.
  2. The engine fetches your pages and measures them. It does not log in, fill in forms, run your scripts beyond what a browser would, or change anything on your site.
  3. A person reads the report. If a finding looks wrong, it is checked against the page before the report leaves us.
  4. You hear from us at the address you gave, with the report and a plain account of what it covers.

A free ten-page run usually comes back within a few working days. A sampled run across a larger site takes longer, and we tell you when we pick it up.

What we keep

Your name, email and organisation stay with your request so we can reply. They are never passed into the audit run, which sees only the address and the pages to read, and they go when the request does. The report is kept for thirty days on the free plan unless you ask for it sooner, and for the length of the engagement otherwise.

The audit measures. It is not a certificate, and it does not make your site compliant with anything. It tells you what a machine can read on the day it ran and what to change.
