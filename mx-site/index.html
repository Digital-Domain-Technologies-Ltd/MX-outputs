Machine Experience (MX) is the practice of adding metadata and instructions to internet assets so AI agents do not have to guess. CogNovaMX provides consultancy, training, books, and tools to help organisations make their websites work for every machine that visits them — from ChatGPT and Claude to Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews, and the thousands of specialised agents emerging across every industry.
