Lifecycle (stage names)

Teach by stage names. Exact button labels stay open until the app cog is refreshed.

  1. Intake / release; website authorisation and scope bound.
  2. Engine posts initial structured report.
  3. Stage Expert review (expert_review). Initial report guides the expert; it cannot be approved or delivered alone.
  4. You edit or supplement and submit a human revision (toward ready).
  5. Staff Approve that exact revision, then Deliver (separate staff steps; second person when available).

Can the engine's initial Expert Review report be Delivered without a human revision?

No. For package human, the initial engine report cannot be approved, published or delivered alone.
Remember package human: mandatory expert revision before Approve or Deliver.
