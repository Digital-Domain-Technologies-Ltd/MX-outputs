How to use these cards

Each pair is a flash card: prompt, then answer when you open it. With scripting on, flip each pair. With scripting off, both sides are visible in document order. No prices. No pitch.

Expert Review vs AX Audit

Prompt: What package is Expert Review? Answer: human (people-led upsell above Free and paid AX Audit)
Prompt: Customer-facing audit name? Answer: AX Audit (never "MX Audit")
Prompt: Is Expert Review "compliance"? Answer: No. Sold as a service; never call AX audits compliance
Prompt: Accredited or certified? Answer: Say accredited, never certified

App, revision, human-in-command

Prompt: Does app.tityra.com assess the site? Answer: No. Audit Suite assesses; app stores and routes
Prompt: Is a human revision required for package human? Answer: Yes. Initial engine report cannot be approved or delivered alone
Prompt: Name one human-in-command check before review Answer: Explain what you authorised; trail names you; machine in scope; you can still tell weak from strong
Prompt: Method in one line Answer: Revise engine AX findings against the live site; not a fresh full-site audit

Checklist A to H

Prompt: Section A? Answer: Safety, bias removal and human-in-command
Prompt: Section B? Answer: Confirm this is Expert Review (not Free / paid alone)
Prompt: Sections C and D? Answer: C Prepare; D Use the app
Prompt: Section E? Answer: Human judgement; revise findings vs live site (E0 duties, E1 criteria)
Prompt: Sections F, G, H? Answer: F Record and submit; G Checklist pass; H Handoff (Approve then Deliver)

E1 and handoff

Prompt: E1.3 tool list? Answer: No named mandatory tool; sense-check the live page; no conformance verdict
Prompt: Submit vs Approve vs Deliver? Answer: Reviewer may submit; Approve and Deliver are separate staff steps; second person when available
Prompt: Does Deliver mean the site was fixed? Answer: No. Remediation is separate; accredited suppliers fix
