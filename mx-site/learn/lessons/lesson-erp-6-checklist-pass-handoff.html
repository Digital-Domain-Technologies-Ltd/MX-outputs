Checklist pass (G)

Walk sections A to F. Complete E0 and E1. Ask the human-in-command question: would you stand behind this revision if a customer asked what you checked?

G1 to G4 pass criteria open
  • A to D complete or N/A with reason
  • E0 and E1 complete or N/A with reason
  • F complete; human revision submitted
  • You would stand behind the revision

Handoff (H): Approve then Deliver

Submit equals customer-visible delivery Staff Approve the exact revision, then Deliver so the customer can see it
Delivery means the site was fixed Remediation is separate; accredited suppliers do fixing
Approve a different revision than the one submitted Approve the exact human revision on the ready request

Prepare, App + live site, Revise, Submit, Checklist pass, Approve, Deliver.
