Expert Review Practice · Module 4 of 6 · Checklist E

Human judgement: revise findings vs live site

Method and E1. Not a fresh full-site audit. Full text remains readable with scripting off.

Method

You are not re-running Audit Suite and not inventing a second full-site audit. You take the engine's initial AX report and revise its findings: deepen thin write-ups, fix priority where the report's resources support it, correct wording, and add missing context. You open the live site (in-scope pages) to verify each finding you stand behind.

Fresh full-site audit from scratch Revise engine AX findings against the live site
Trust the engine write-up without opening the page Open cited in-scope live pages for findings you deliver
Write a conformance or "certified" verdict Accessibility sense-check; evidence-shaped wording only

Open all three to continue.

E1 criteria (checkable)

Duties from the handoff: website investigation, accessibility sense-check, deeper review; plus claim advisories, confidence vs evidence, and human-in-command. No named accessibility tool is mandated (E1.3 as written).

E1.1 to E1.3 · Scope and sense-check open
  • E1.1 Not a fresh audit: revise existing findings.
  • E1.2 Website investigation: open cited live pages for findings you deliver.
  • E1.3 Accessibility sense-check on the live page; no conformance verdict; any check available to you is fine.
E1.4 to E1.6 · Deeper review open
  • E1.4 Depth / missing context.
  • E1.5 Priority.
  • E1.6 Wording: evidence-shaped; no "compliance"; no "certified".
E1.7 to E1.9 · Advisories, confidence, stand-behind open
  • E1.7 Resolve every claim advisory.
  • E1.8 High confidence only with evidence and a named resource.
  • E1.9 Human-in-command: name what you checked and changed.

Open all three groups to continue.

Quick check

E1.3 requires which of these?

E1.3 is checkable without naming a product. Try again.

What you now know

  • Revise findings; do not replace the report with a from-scratch audit.
  • E1.1 to E1.9 are the checkable criteria.
  • Testing how agents complete tasks on a site is out of scope: it is a separate service in the audit suite.
