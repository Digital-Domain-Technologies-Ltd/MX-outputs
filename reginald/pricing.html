Five Compliance Levels

Not every document needs the same level of provenance. A company's internal wiki has different requirements than a pharmaceutical dosage reference.

LevelNameRequirementsUse Case
1 — BasicYAML present, publisher identifiedInternal documentationQuick-start adoption
2 — StructuredMX-compliant format, maintainer + contactPublic documentationProfessionalised content
3 — AttestedCryptographically attested, review cycle + triggersREGINALD minimumCommercial documentation
4 — RegisteredAttested + registered, full contract with SLAEnterprise documentationRegulated industries
5 — AuditedAttested + registered + third-party provenancedCritical documentationHealthcare, finance, legal

Level 3 is the minimum for REGINALD registration — the point where AI systems can trust the content programmatically. Level 5 is for industries where a wrong answer does not just waste tokens — it harms people.
