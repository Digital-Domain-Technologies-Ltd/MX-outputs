## What Was Done

### 1. VC Magazine backgrounder - quality pass and content additions

The Xandra Ramos interview backgrounder went through a full humanizer pass (19 deterministic fixes: negation-pivot collapses, verbal tics removed, AI vocabulary swapped out) and three content additions. A new section was added - "MX is not SEO, GEO, or accessibility compliance" - drawn from the existing blog post at mx-a-new-role.html, making the relationship between MX and adjacent disciplines explicit for a general tech-media audience. The accessibility framing was broadened: the backgrounder previously said "ADA" in a document headed for a global audience. It now reads "Accessibility compliance - the ADA in the United States, the European Accessibility Act in the EU, and WCAG-based legislation elsewhere." The further-reading links were made fully clickable (title as link plus printed URL), a footer image added as a closure, and a REGINALD footer link included. The PDF regenerated with an MX Compatible badge.

### 2. General MX introduction document

The backgrounder was copied to the business folder and stripped of all interview-specific prose to become "An Introduction to MX" - a standalone document that is not pitched at regulated industries, not framed as press prep, and usable for any inbound prospect. It has its own PDF, its own provenance sidecars, and its own canonical URI. The regulated-industries framing in the backgrounder stays intact; the general document broadens to any organisation that publishes content.

### 3. Services introduction page - card and zone layout

A new page was created at mx.allabout.network/services/introduction.html. The first version used alternating dark/mid prose sections. Tom asked for cards and zones; the main content was rewritten as seven distinct zones (hero, what MX is not, two pillars, employment example, regulatory wave, BBC origin, further reading) each using the proposition-grid/proposition-card pattern already established across the services section. The services index page gained a "New to MX? Start here" card pointing to the new page. A markdown source pointer was added to the Intent CMS source layer.

### 4. Agency white-label one-pager and blog posts

The agency white-label service - where an agency can deliver MX-branded audits under their own brand and keep the engagement revenue - was written up as a formal one-pager with a client-retention framing and an August 2026 regulatory trigger. The PDF is ready for the next agency prospect conversation. Two related blog posts were drafted: the agency machine-readiness opportunity (chatty Tom voice, targeting agency readers) and the employment agency AI letters use case (the NYC LL144 concrete example). Both are in the Intent CMS source layer as drafts.

### 5. Pitch file updates

All four primary pitch files were updated in one pass: training was added as a distinct revenue line (not folded into books), the Human-in-Command data-sovereign product was named as a forward roadmap item in the investor and team pitches, and the August 2026 EU AI Act Article 50 deadline was added as a regulatory tailwind to the investor and team pitches. ## The Insight

The services introduction page went through two iterations in one session: first as long prose sections, then as cards. The card layout is strictly better for this kind of "what is this and why does it matter" content - a reader can scan to the zone they care about (two pillars, regulatory, further reading) without reading the whole page. The proposition-grid pattern already existed across the services section; applying it here was pattern reuse, not new design work.
