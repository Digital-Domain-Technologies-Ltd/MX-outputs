## Executive Summary

The London CMS Experts lightning talk is ready. Tom wanted controversy — we delivered it.

The presentation centres on a provocative thesis: **"Headless CMS made you AI-blind."** The argument is that the architectural pattern CMS practitioners have celebrated for the past five years — decoupling the CMS from the front-end — has inadvertently created a blind spot. The front-end teams who now own the HTML are optimising for developer experience, not machine readability. React, Vue, Next.js — beautiful for developers, opaque to AI agents.

This is deliberately confrontational for a CMS practitioner audience. Some will bristle. Good. They'll remember Tom.

The presentation exists in three formats (PPTX, PDF, Marp source), with detailed speaker notes and delivery coaching for the controversy moment.

## The Controversy Thesis

**Slide 2 content:**

- You decoupled your CMS from the **publication point**
- The front-end team owns the HTML now
- They're optimising for **DX**, not machine readability
- React, Vue, Next.js — beautiful for developers, **opaque to AI agents**

**Provocation line:** *"The trendiest thing you did in the last 5 years may be the reason AI agents can't understand your site."*

This is designed to land with impact in a room of CMS practitioners who have been championing headless architectures. It challenges their assumptions while remaining technically accurate — the separation of concerns that headless enables has often meant that nobody explicitly owns the machine-readable output.
