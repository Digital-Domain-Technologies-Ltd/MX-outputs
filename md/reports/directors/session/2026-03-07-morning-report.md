## The Strategic Context

The messaging file audit confirmed that most of the 18 files serve genuinely distinct purposes — different audiences, different depths, different narrative approaches. The investor-pitch (financial/analytical) and vision-brief-raskin (emotional/narrative) are deliberately different strategies for different decision-maker types. The seven audience one-pagers each tailor language for a specific fear profile.

The consolidation principle: if two documents say the same thing to the same audience at different lengths, keep one and merge the unique content. If they serve different audiences or use different persuasion structures, they are complementary, not duplicates.

- **Deliverables that are messaging reference materials live in ssot** — not in the authoring workspace
