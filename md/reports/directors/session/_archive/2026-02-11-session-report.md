## The Insight

Tom articulated two framing shifts that change how MX communicates to non-technical audiences:

**Before:** "The action-cog IS the prompt. Any LLM is the runtime."
**After:** "The action-cog IS the Standard Operating Procedure. Any LLM follows it."

**Before:** "An info-cog is a data file."
**After:** "An info-cog is a single source of truth — the verified, authoritative answer to any question an AI agent asks."

These are not cosmetic changes. The word "prompt" positions action-cogs as AI artefacts — disposable, technical, unfamiliar. The word "SOP" positions them as operational infrastructure — governed, audited, essential. Every operations manager, every compliance officer, every QA lead knows what an SOP is. Now they know what an action-cog is.
