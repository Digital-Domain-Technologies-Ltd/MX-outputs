## What Changed

### Investment Position

Investment ask increased from £206,000-£341,000 to **£500,000**. Justified by expanded scope: MX App development, MX View engine, lift-and-shift service infrastructure, Frankfurt demo, and 18 months of runway.

### Product Definition

The MX App is now defined as a **web browser** that generates **The Machine Experience** — personalised replacement pages. Two modes: Cog mode (personalised) and Cleanup mode (strip dark patterns). Frankfurt demo: Android phone + three Macs.

## Decisions Made

1. **The Machine Experience** is the defining concept of MX. The MX App generates personalised replacement pages from cog data.
2. **Two modes** — Cog mode for sites with metadata, Cleanup mode for all others.
3. **Privacy paradox is the core pitch** — personalisation without privacy intrusion.
4. **Consent-as-a-cog** — GDPR preferences set once, applied everywhere.
5. **Android for demo** — Tauri v2 iOS is unstable. Android + three Macs.
6. **Content that describes itself doesn't need a manager** — the CMS is a transitional technology.
7. **Five-generation evolution** — static → database → headless → composable → cogs.
8. **Self-disruption** — "The AEM Guy" to "The MX Guy" is the proof of conviction.
9. **£500K investment** — increased from £206K-£341K to cover expanded scope.
10. **MX Vision 2026** — new Canon initiative holding the definitive investor pitch.
