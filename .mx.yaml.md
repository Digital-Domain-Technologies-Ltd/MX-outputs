# MX Outputs

## Purpose and Role

Personal build artefacts repository. Contains all generated outputs organised by type: markdown reports, HTML pages, PDFs, presentations, JSON data.

This is a **personal mount** — each business deploying MX generates their own outputs into their own repo.

## Mount Type

**Personal** — swappable per business. Tom's outputs are his reports, his PDFs, his audit baselines. Another business would mount their own outputs repo.
